    Notes on the data
    • Average daily population (ADP) is the denominator for every rate here. Through June 2019 it comes from a Vital City compilation of DOC monthly figures that matches the Mayor's Management Report's fiscal-year ADP; from July 2019 it is rebuilt from Open Data by counting everyone in custody each day. The rebuild runs within about 1% of the official fiscal-year figures.
    • Year-to-date rates compare the same months in every year. When incident data run past the latest month of ADP, the most recent ADP is used.
    • Use of force is reported by fiscal year only, so that chart is labeled FY and updates once a year.
    • Length of stay before 2014 comes from an earlier Vital City chart built on a different source; the Open Data series starts in 2014.
